# NORDVELL Brand Standard

Version: 1.0. Applicable to NORDVELL email, Content Library, and Decisioning offer content. This fictional brand system was created for the Beat the Buzzer scenario. Campaign facts still require explicit approval.

## Brand Idea

NORDVELL makes quiet, capable clothing for weather in motion. The identity combines Nordic restraint with useful outdoor detail: calm rather than cold, premium rather than precious, technical without jargon.

Always write the wordmark as `NORDVELL`. Never abbreviate it to NORD. Use a text wordmark until an approved image asset is supplied.

## Visual System

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight | `#101714` | Primary text, header, dark surfaces |
| Lichen | `#BFD76D` | Controlled accent and availability signal |
| Fjord | `#406A64` | Secondary surface and supporting graphics |
| Cloud | `#F2F1EA` | Warm background |
| Snow | `#FFFFFF` | Content surfaces and reverse text |
| Granite | `#626862` | Secondary text |
| Mist | `#D8DDD5` | Borders and quiet dividers |
| Alert | `#A34132` | Errors and unavailable states only |

Maintain WCAG AA contrast for normal text. Never use Lichen as body text on Cloud or Snow. Prefer large editorial photography, restrained color blocks, fine rules, and generous space over gradients, shadows, glass effects, pills, or decorative badges.

Email typography is Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif. Use medium weights, tight display tracking, sentence case for headlines, and uppercase only for the wordmark and short labels. Use an 8px spacing rhythm, 0-4px corner radius, and buttons at least 44px high.

Photography is natural, tactile, lightly desaturated, and editorial. Avoid third-party branding, artificial grading, fake reviews, crowded collages, or imagery that conflicts with the product facts.

## Voice

Use calm, specific, compact language. Lead with the useful truth, then the verified option. Prefer one thought per sentence, active voice, and concrete nouns. Headlines usually contain two to seven words. Body paragraphs contain one to three short sentences. CTA labels begin with a verb and identify the destination.

Preferred terms include `your size`, `available`, `returning`, `near you`, `another option`, `notify me`, `view`, `explore`, and `check availability` when supported by evidence.

Avoid hype, apology theatre, exclamation marks, vague luxury language, technical inventory jargon, and manipulative urgency. Do not use `exclusive`, `selected for you`, `selling fast`, `last chance`, `best`, `guaranteed`, `sustainable`, or `free` unless the exact claim and conditions are supplied and approved.

Never expose algorithms, audiences, segments, policies, reports, synthetic data, or internal offer roles in customer copy.

## Truthful Content Roles

`TARGETED_BACK_IN_STOCK` is an internal role. Use it only when the recipient context and at least one recovery option are supported: confirmed replenishment or availability of the awaited size, a genuine same-size alternative, or verified local stock. Name a size only from the approved audience/profile context. Include only modules whose facts are verified.

`FALLBACK_NOTIFY_ONLY` is an internal role. Use it when no verified recovery option exists. It may acknowledge interest and offer notification, but must not imply replenishment timing, local stock, an alternative, exclusivity, or priority access.

Internal role names never appear in customer-facing content.

## Claims and Approvals

Product prices, availability, replenishment, pickup, market, eligibility, discounts, delivery, returns, composition, performance, environmental claims, legal copy, URLs, and assets are campaign facts. Supply and approve them independently of this brand standard.

Never invent savings, original prices, codes, scarcity, popularity, testimonials, ratings, awards, partnerships, stock, urgency, superiority, guarantees, performance, sustainability, legal terms, or destinations.

Brand alignment does not approve legal language, offer economics, eligibility, assets, accessibility conformance, Adobe mutations, proofs, activation, delivery, or measured impact.
